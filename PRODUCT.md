# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective graduate students and research interns, academic collaborators, researchers, and visitors evaluating MAIR Lab's work at HUST.

## Product Purpose

Present the lab's research identity, latest news, recruitment information, and publications in a credible bilingual academic homepage. Success means visitors can understand the lab quickly, inspect substantive work, and find the right contact path in either English or Simplified Chinese.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, clear, authored. The site should feel calm and research-led, with visual confidence coming from real project imagery and precise editorial structure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative gradients, heavy shadows, nested cards, crowded navigation, novelty interactions, and visual treatments that compete with research figures or long-form academic content.

## Design Principles

1. Make evidence visible through real figures, publications, and specific lab information.
2. Keep information architecture identical across languages so switching never disorients visitors.
3. Prefer direct controls and short labels over menus when the choice set is small.
4. Preserve generous whitespace, strong type hierarchy, and predictable reading order.
5. Treat accessibility and responsive behavior as part of the visual system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic language metadata, readable contrast, reduced-motion preferences, responsive layouts, and complete English and Simplified Chinese content parity.
