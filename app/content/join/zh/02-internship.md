实验室长期招募多模态智能、具身智能和 AI4S 方向的优秀本科生、硕士生、博士生实习生 / The lab welcomes undergraduate, master's, and doctoral research interns interested in multimodal intelligence, embodied intelligence, and AI4S.

**研究主题 / Research Topics:**

- 多模态生成与理解 / Multimodal Generation and Understanding
- 世界模型与具身智能 / World Models and Embodied Intelligence
- 科学智能 / AI for Science (AI4S)

**你将获得 / You Will Gain:**

- 实验室成员的一对一科研指导与相关研究资源 / One-on-one research guidance and research resources
- 参与聚焦型研究项目并争取论文发表机会 / Opportunities to join focused projects and pursue publications
- 在合作中提升科研、工程实现与学术沟通能力 / Growth in research, engineering, and academic communication skills

实习可根据项目与指导安排在线下或远程进行 / Internships may be on-site or remote, depending on the project and supervision arrangement.

请将个人简历、代表性成果、研究兴趣和预计实习周期发送至 mzyth@hust.edu.cn / Please send your CV, representative work, research interests, and expected internship period to mzyth@hust.edu.cn.

邮件标题请注明“科研实习申请-学校-姓名” / Use the subject line “Research Internship Application - University - Name”.
