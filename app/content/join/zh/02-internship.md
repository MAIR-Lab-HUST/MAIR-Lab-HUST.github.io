实验室长期招募多模态智能、具身智能、世界模型和 AI4S 方向的优秀本科生、硕士生、博士生实习生 / The lab welcomes outstanding undergraduate, master's, and doctoral students for long-term research internships in multimodal intelligence, embodied intelligence, world models, and AI for Science (AI4S).

**研究主题 / Research Topics:**

- **图像、视频生成&理解大模型 / Large Models for Image and Video Generation & Understanding**
- **多模态大模型后训练（RL、OPD等） / Post-Training of Multimodal Large Models (RL, OPD, etc.)**
- **具身智能与世界模型（特别关注） / Embodied Intelligence and World Models (Special Focus)**
- **科学智能（AI4S） / AI for Science (AI4S)**

**你将获得 / You Will Gain:**

- 实验室导师、成员的一对一科研指导与相关研究资源 / One-on-one research guidance from the lab's faculty and members, along with relevant research resources
- 直接 hands on 参与实际研究项目并发表论文 / Hands-on involvement in real research projects and opportunities to publish papers
- 表现优秀的同学可以获得大厂实习和知名高校的推荐机会 / Outstanding students may receive recommendations for internships at leading tech companies and further study at prestigious universities
- 在合作中提升科研、工程实现与学术沟通能力 / Opportunities to strengthen your research, engineering implementation, and academic communication skills through collaboration

**华中科技大学本科学生特别说明 / Special Notice for HUST Undergraduate Students:**

- 非常欢迎对实验室方向感兴趣的大一大二学生，以及想要保研的大三学生进组实习，边干边学是最快的科研进步方法 / We especially welcome first- and second-year students interested in the lab's research areas, as well as third-year students preparing for graduate recommendation, to join us as interns. Learning by doing is the fastest way to grow as a researcher
- 实验室能提供给本科生直接上手实际科研项目、并发表 Paper 的机会 / The lab offers undergraduate students opportunities to work directly on real research projects and publish papers
- 华中科技大学学生可约时间到计算机新大楼6024办公室面聊 / HUST students may schedule an in-person discussion at Room 6024 in the new Computer Science Building

**工作地点选择 / Location Options:**

- 华中科技大学主校区（湖北，武汉）（华科的学生建议线下实习，会安排工位） / HUST Main Campus (Wuhan, Hubei). HUST students are encouraged to intern on site, and workspace will be provided
