**招生方向 / Research Areas:**

- **图像、视频生成&理解大模型 / Large Models for Image and Video Generation & Understanding**
- **多模态大模型后训练（RL、OPD等） / Post-Training of Multimodal Large Models (RL, OPD, etc.)**
- **具身智能与世界模型（特别关注） / Embodied Intelligence and World Models (Special Focus)**
- **科学智能（AI4S） / AI for Science (AI4S)**

**发展机会 / Opportunities:**

- 实验室与上海 AI Lab、快手、腾讯、擎羽科技等业内知名企业和实验室都有合作，并且合作单位正在逐渐增加中，表现优秀者可推荐大厂实习 / The lab collaborates with leading companies and research labs, including Shanghai AI Laboratory, Kuaishou, Tencent, and Qingyu Technology, with more partnerships underway. Outstanding students may be recommended for internships at leading tech companies
- 可推荐到知名高校深造 / Recommendations for further study at prestigious universities

**申请要求 / Requirements:**

- 背景不限，能力优先 / No restrictions on academic background; ability and potential come first
- 实验室不打卡，要求主动性强，有较强的自我驱动力 / The lab has no attendance clock-in requirement, but students should be proactive and highly self-motivated
- 好奇心强，对新技术有强烈探索欲望 / Strong curiosity and enthusiasm for exploring new technologies
- 对多模态智能、具身智能有浓厚兴趣 / Strong interest in multimodal intelligence and embodied intelligence
- 良好的编程能力（Python/C++） / Good programming skills in Python and/or C++
- 英语读写能力良好 / Good English reading and writing skills
