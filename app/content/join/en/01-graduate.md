**Research Areas:**

- **Large Models for Image and Video Generation & Understanding**
- **Post-Training of Multimodal Large Models (RL, OPD, etc.)**
- **Embodied Intelligence and World Models (Special Focus)**
- **AI for Science (AI4S)**

**Opportunities:**

- The lab collaborates with leading companies and research labs, including Shanghai AI Laboratory, Kuaishou, Tencent, and Qingyu Technology, with more partnerships underway. Outstanding students may be recommended for internships at leading tech companies
- Recommendations for further study at prestigious universities

**Requirements:**

- No restrictions on academic background; ability and potential come first
- The lab has no attendance clock-in requirement, but students should be proactive and highly self-motivated
- Strong curiosity and enthusiasm for exploring new technologies
- Strong interest in multimodal intelligence and embodied intelligence
- Good programming skills in Python and/or C++
- Good English reading and writing skills
