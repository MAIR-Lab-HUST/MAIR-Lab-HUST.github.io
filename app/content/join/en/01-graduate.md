We welcome applications and inquiries for Fall graduate study starting in 2027 or later. After joining the team, you will explore challenging research problems in multimodal intelligence, embodied intelligence, and AI4S.

- **Strong academic preparation.** Solid foundations in computer science, mathematics, or related disciplines; prior research is a plus.
- **Passion and perseverance.** A willingness to tackle difficult problems persistently, with creativity and patience in the face of uncertainty in research.
- **Self-motivated learning.** The ability to quickly enter new research directions and master the tools and methods they require.
- **Programming and communication.** Strong engineering and implementation skills, as well as sufficient English for academic reading, writing, and collaboration.

Please send your CV, published papers or other representative work, and a research statement (if available) to the lab email (mzyth@hust.edu.cn). Please also explain how your research interests or previous work align with our research directions.

Please use the subject line “Application for [Year] Master’s/Direct-Entry PhD · University · Name,” where [Year] is your intended year of enrollment. If your background and interests appear to match our directions, the professor will reply to invite you for an interview.
