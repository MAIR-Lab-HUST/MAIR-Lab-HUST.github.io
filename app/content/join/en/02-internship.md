The lab continuously recruits outstanding undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral students with a foundation in multimodal and embodied intelligence for research internships. Applicants with hands-on experience with embodied hardware are especially welcome. Depending on the project and supervision arrangement, internships may be conducted on-site or remotely.

**Research topics:** Multimodal generation and understanding · World models and embodied intelligence · AI4S

**As a research intern, you will:** Receive one-on-one research guidance from lab members and access to relevant research resources. Participate in focused research projects and pursue opportunities to publish high-level papers. Develop your research, engineering, and academic communication skills through close collaboration.

Please send your CV, published papers or other representative work to the lab email (mzyth@hust.edu.cn). Please also describe your research interests and your expected joining date or internship period. For research internship applications, use the subject line “Research Internship Application · University · Name.”
