The lab welcomes outstanding undergraduate, master's, and doctoral students for long-term research internships in multimodal intelligence, embodied intelligence, world models, and AI for Science (AI4S).

**Research Topics:**

- **Large Models for Image and Video Generation & Understanding**
- **Post-Training of Multimodal Large Models (RL, OPD, etc.)**
- **Embodied Intelligence and World Models (Special Focus)**
- **AI for Science (AI4S)**

**You Will Gain:**

- One-on-one research guidance from the lab's faculty and members, along with relevant research resources
- Hands-on involvement in real research projects and opportunities to publish papers
- Outstanding students may receive recommendations for internships at leading tech companies and further study at prestigious universities
- Opportunities to strengthen your research, engineering implementation, and academic communication skills through collaboration

**Special Notice for HUST Undergraduate Students:**

- We especially welcome first- and second-year students interested in the lab's research areas, as well as third-year students preparing for graduate recommendation, to join us as interns. Learning by doing is the fastest way to grow as a researcher
- The lab offers undergraduate students opportunities to work directly on real research projects and publish papers
- HUST students may schedule an in-person discussion at Room 6024 in the new Computer Science Building

**Location Options:**

- HUST Main Campus (Wuhan, Hubei). HUST students are encouraged to intern on site, and workspace will be provided
- Remote
