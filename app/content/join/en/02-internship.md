The lab welcomes undergraduate, master's, and doctoral research interns interested in multimodal intelligence, embodied intelligence, and AI4S / 实验室长期招募相关方向的优秀本科生、硕士生、博士生实习生。

**Research Topics / 研究主题:**

- Multimodal Generation and Understanding / 多模态生成与理解
- World Models and Embodied Intelligence / 世界模型与具身智能
- AI for Science (AI4S) / 科学智能

**You Will Gain / 你将获得:**

- One-on-one research guidance and research resources / 一对一科研指导与研究资源
- Opportunities to join focused projects and pursue publications / 参与研究项目并争取论文发表机会
- Growth in research, engineering, and academic communication skills / 提升科研、工程实现与学术沟通能力

Internships may be on-site or remote / 实习可在线下或远程进行。

Please send your CV, representative work, research interests, and expected internship period to mzyth@hust.edu.cn / 请将材料发送至 mzyth@hust.edu.cn。

Use the subject line “Research Internship Application - University - Name” / 邮件标题请注明“科研实习申请-学校-姓名”。
